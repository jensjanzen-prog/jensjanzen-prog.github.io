Personal Policies & Procedures

These policies exist to ensure fairness, consistency, and the orderly processing of matters that should probably have been simple.

Important Administrative Notice

Policies are applied uniformly. Regrettably, this uniformity may include you.

Policy 01

Refund Processing Fee

Refund requests are subject to a non-refundable administrative processing fee of US$27.99, regardless of the amount refunded or the outcome of the request.

Policy 02

Exception Requests

Requests for exceptions require submission of Form EX-14B. The form is available by email request and requires payment of a non-refundable administrative fee of US$27.99, regardless of outcome.

Policy 03

Invoicing of Services

All services rendered, including but not limited to medical procedures, consultations, lawn mowing, landscaping, construction, and renovation projects, shall be invoiced within three (3) months of delivery. After this period, any non-invoiced service will be considered a gift or donation from the service provider to Jens Janzen, with the service provider abandoning all rights to future compensation, monetary or otherwise.

Policy 04

Urgent Matters

If a matter is deemed urgent, it may be properly identified as such, in which case it will be ignored with our highest level of priority. Unfortunately, this may cause delays in resolution. We hope you understand.

Policy 05

Complaints

Complaints must be submitted in triplicate and marked as urgent. Otherwise, they will be regarded as mere “venting” and deemed to have fulfilled their purpose through their mere existence. Electronic filings are acceptable and will be processed in the same manner.

Policy 06

Policy Changes

These policies may be amended, clarified, renumbered, or applied retroactively whenever doing so improves administrative efficiency.
